# M6.9F.4D — LIVE Inventory and Supplier

## Scope and source of truth

This adds LIVE wrappers for Inventory and Supplier over the validated immutable
`LiveV3Context`. Historical requests (`asOfDate <= 2026-10-12`) continue through
the existing M6.6/M6.8 runtime path. The implementation does not change Business
or Customer adapters, V1/V2 serving, forecast models, or any persistence path.

For LIVE on-hand stock, the only source is `stockStates` validated as
`MONGO_LEDGER` by the backend snapshot contract. Frozen ending stock and
movement reconstruction are not used. Sale cancellations are already reflected
by the backend ledger; analytics do not apply a second stock adjustment or
delete/alter movements. Consequently a sale, receive, or cancellation appears
only after the corresponding new snapshot/revision is supplied.

## Inventory behavior

The adapter reuses M6.6 helpers and its frozen daily observations for ABC/XYZ
and observable-history indicators. It overlays only complete LIVE closed-sale
days from the snapshot. New SKUs stay `PROVISIONAL`; missing historical cost,
availability, or sufficient history remains explicitly unavailable instead of
being converted to zero or an inferred class.

Incoming quantities are the remaining quantity of active deferred purchases
with a known promised local date strictly after the LIVE cutoff. Missing or past
promise dates are reported separately as overdue/unknown expected arrival.
Canceled purchases and immediate purchases are excluded from incoming. A
RECEIVE reduces the remaining quantity; on-hand is read from the same snapshot's
ledger state, so received units are not added a second time.

LIVE forecast is not enabled until F.4E. Accordingly demand, days cover,
forecast-derived risk, calculated safety stock/reorder point, reorder
recommendation, and overstock are unavailable (`LIVE_FORECAST_REQUIRED`). The
catalog's configured minimum/safety/reorder values are exposed as configured
values, distinct from unavailable calculated LIVE policy. Current physical
stock, configured minimum comparison, known incoming, and supplier-offer
dependency can still be reported. Queries filtered/ranked by forecast-derived
risk return a partial result with an unavailable count, not a false empty
success. Slow/dead indicators are null when their observation-coverage gates
are not met.

ABC uses the approved historical 365-day consumption-value rule plus LIVE
observed sale-line cost. If required cost evidence is incomplete, classes remain
unavailable; newly created products are provisional. XYZ uses the approved M6.6
calculation with only the 13 complete Monday-Sunday weeks before the current
operational week; it requires at least eight sufficiently observed weeks and
does not treat unknown availability as zero demand.

## Supplier behavior

M6.8 historical supplier aggregates at the frozen cutoff are preserved, then
explicit LIVE purchase and RECEIVE evidence is overlaid. Pending orders count
as ordered/pending and committed value, but not received units/spend, lead-time
samples, or punctuality outcomes. Immediate purchases are acquired spend and
received units but do not create lead-time or punctuality samples. A dated full
receipt uses the explicit RECEIVE event time and order-captured unit cost;
current product cost is never substituted. Costs are labeled with their
provenance (`ORDER_COST`, `HISTORICAL_COST`, or `UNAVAILABLE` as supported by
the available evidence). New suppliers can expose orders, commitment, and
pending status while lead-time/punctuality remain `INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY`.

The present snapshot schema permits RECEIVE only for a purchase represented in
the LIVE snapshot. Receiving an older frozen pending purchase is therefore not
yet supported by this adapter contract. The RECEIVE schema also carries ordered
cost but not a separate observed receipt unit-cost field; it cannot claim
`OBSERVED_RECEIPT_COST` without that evidence. These limitations must be resolved
by a future contract phase, not inferred here.

Supplier `purchaseFrequency` applies the requested inclusive period to frozen
and LIVE order dates (or defaults to the LIVE delta window). Other supplier
aggregates remain lifetime-as-of metrics as identified by `metricsWindow`. If
LIVE lead observations are merged with M6.8 historical aggregates, the weighted
mean and sample count are retained, but historical median/p90/deviation are
cleared because raw frozen samples are not available for an exact merge.

Supplier dependency/offers are reported from the current catalog projection or
approved frozen offer data; no supplier score/rating is manufactured. Forecast-
dependent critical exposure remains unavailable until F.4E.

## Cache and freshness

The adapter uses a bounded worker-local read-only projection cache. Keys include
business, scenario, `dataVersion`, snapshot hash, `asOfDate`, domain, engine
version, and request filters/period. A new revision or changed snapshot cannot
reuse an earlier projection. Cache access/eviction is protected with a lock;
cached base projections are not mutated while shaping responses. Each worker
may hold its own bounded cache; no cross-process singleton is assumed for
correctness. LIVE response freshness carries snapshot version and hash from the
validated context.

## HTTP and readiness

The existing authenticated routes are enabled additively:

- `POST /v3/intelligence/inventory`
- `POST /v3/intelligence/suppliers`

They accept the existing strict InventoryRequest/SupplierRequest and LIVE
snapshot envelope. Missing snapshots still fail with `LIVE_SNAPSHOT_REQUIRED`.
Business and Customer remain ready. Forecast and Business Health remain
`LIVE_ANALYTICS_NOT_READY`; this phase does not enable F.4E.

## Validation

F.4D focused tests (16/16) cover ledger stock, sale cancellation, RECEIVE without double
counting, pending and canceled purchases, new suppliers, cost provenance,
forecast-required partial results, 13 full XYZ weeks, cache revision isolation,
direct-adapter/HTTP parity, missing snapshot behavior, and additive readiness.
Related F.3/F.4B/F.4C, M6.6, M6.8, serving-performance, and V1/V2 regressions are
run before commit. Tests use local fixtures/TestClient only; no Atlas, cloud,
real LIVE data, deployment, or writes are involved.

Performance is measured only on the tiny synthetic fixture (not a production
SLA): direct adapter first/warm median/p90/max was inventory 0.142/0.015/0.021/
0.032 ms and supplier 0.065/0.019/0.022/0.029 ms. These figures demonstrate cache
reuse only and must not be interpreted as full-dataset or cloud latency. The
adapter builds one per-snapshot domain projection and then serves selected
SKU/rank/filter views from it; no benchmark-scale dataset run is part of this
phase. Related F.3/F.4B/F.4C, M6.6, M6.8, serving-performance, and V1/V2
regressions passed 100/100 in the local fixture suite.

## F.4E blockers

F.4E must provide the approved LIVE forecast inputs and calculations before
enabling days cover, stockout risk, forecast-based safety stock/reorder point,
recommended quantity, overstock, dependency exposure rankings, or LIVE Health.
Until then, those fields remain explicitly partial/unavailable.
