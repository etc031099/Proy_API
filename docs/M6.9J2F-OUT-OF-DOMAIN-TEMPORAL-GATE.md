# M6.9J.2F — Temporal sports questions and the domain gate

## Observed case

The question “quien juega mañana?” was not recognized by the deterministic out-of-domain gate. It therefore continued through normal routing; an unresolved request could reach tool selection and be misinterpreted as a product forecast. The temporal word itself was not enough to trigger the forecast temporal router: direct reproduction returned no deterministic plan for the sports questions.

## Root cause and fix

The gate recognized a small fixed set of external questions (including “quien gano el partido ayer”) but did not cover sports questions about who plays, what match is on, or who won. Added a narrow, temporal sports-question pattern before fallback routing. Existing business-scope terms still take precedence, so sales, stock, product, customer, supplier, and business questions continue through business routing.

The forecast parser, model, formulas, and temporal semantics were not changed. Forecast remains selected by existing forecast/business language, not by a bare date or weekday.

## Regression coverage

Sports questions checked as OUT_OF_DOMAIN:

- quien juega mañana
- qué partido hay mañana
- quién juega hoy
- qué partidos hay esta semana
- quién juega el domingo
- quién ganó ayer

For each, the orchestrator test verifies the OOD response occurs before entity lookup, provider invocation, or business-skill execution (zero calls, tokens, and skills).

Business-domain controls remain outside the OOD gate, including sales tomorrow, forecast for one day, sales for the last seven days, and the requested forecast phrasings for tomorrow/Sprite/bread. Existing temporal forecast tests continue to pass.
